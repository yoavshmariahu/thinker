System behaviors
The rules every change must keep

A behavior says what your system must always (or never) do, and where the code upholds it. thinker review warns about a change that breaks one, and stops the change when the behavior is blocking. They come from the design documents checked into the repository, warn only until you make one blocking, and are yours to edit or discard. Proposed behaviors are not requirements until you accept them.
