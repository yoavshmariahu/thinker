Report 9, two complaints about the traces command line. `mlflow traces delete --trace-ids "tr-1, tr-2"` (a space after the comma) passes the second id on with its leading space, so that trace is never found. And `mlflow traces search --order-by "timestamp_ms DESC, status"` passes " status" with its leading space as the second ordering. Both should behave as if the spaces around each entry were not there.

Reproduce, fix. Reply with one line per complaint.
