Net so far
Every call sends the whole conversation. New tokens are written into the prompt cache once; everything before them is read again. At Claude Opus 5.5 list prices a write is $8 a million tokens, a read 20¢.
Twenty cents a million sounds cheap. But the context grows with every tool result and every call reads all of it, so the read line grows on every call while the write line stays flat.
Nine calls in, re-reading costs 3¢ a call. Forty calls on it is 8¢, more than the new tokens cost, and $2.53 has gone on reading the same context again.
thinker prices every turn: dropping the old results would make every later read smaller, but the prompt after the cut has to be written again. It cuts when that write pays for itself within 20 calls.
The old results are trimmed from the conversation thinker forwards. The newest turn and the last three results stay; prompts, call text and thinking are never touched.
Call 10 takes the hit: the 40k after the cut is written again at $8 a million, 32¢ on top of the call. The uncut call is the outline behind it.
Every call after reads 106k less: 2¢ off each one. The write is repaid by call 24, and from there every call is net saving.
By call 49 the cut has returned 1.7× its cost: 53¢ less on these forty calls, an eighth of their context bill at list prices in this example. What a real session saved is in thinker proxy report.
